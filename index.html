Privacy Policy for Where Parked

Last updated: 2026-09-07

Overview

Where Parked ("the App", package com.myhome.whereparked) helps you remember parking places, zones, time limits, photos, notes, and reminders. The App is for personal organization only. This policy explains what data is processed and how.

Local Storage

The App stores the data you enter locally on your device, including parking place notes, zones, levels, time limits, dates, photos, reminder settings, language settings, and export preferences.

We do not operate a server for this App and do not receive these local records.

Location

The current build does not request Android fine or coarse location permission. If you type location notes manually, they remain local on your device unless you export or share them yourself.

Photos and Export

Optional photos are stored in the App's local private storage. If the App lets you export a text copy of your records, you choose where that exported data is sent or saved using Android sharing tools.

Advertising (AdMob)

Free users may see ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may process device and usage data under Google's privacy terms. Where required, the App may show consent flows for ads.

Google Privacy Policy

Optional Usage Analytics (Firebase)

Usage analytics is off by default and starts only if you opt in. You can turn it off at any time in Settings. When enabled, the App uses Google Analytics for Firebase to measure app lifecycle and purchase events plus four product-health actions: creating a first useful record, scheduling a first reminder, opening a local notification, and creating a first template where templates are supported.

The App never adds your entered names, notes, dates, photos, categories, locations, medicine or gift details, or other record contents to analytics events. Firebase may process an app-instance identifier, basic app and device metadata, purchase product metadata, and coarse location derived from a masked IP address. Analytics Advertising ID collection and ad-personalization signals are disabled by the App. Analytics data is encrypted in transit.

Turning analytics off stops future analytics collection from the App. Previously collected data is handled under Google Analytics retention and deletion controls. See Firebase Privacy and Security and Google Privacy Policy.

In-App Purchases

The App may offer in-app purchases through Google Play Billing, for example to remove ads. Purchase state is checked with Google Play to determine active entitlements.

Notifications

If enabled by you, the App uses local notifications and alarms for reminders. On Android 13+, notification permission is requested by the system.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Play Billing (payments)
  • Google AdMob (ads)
  • Google Analytics for Firebase (optional usage analytics)
  • Google User Messaging Platform (ad consent, where applicable)

Data Deletion

You can remove your data by deleting records and photos inside the App, clearing app data in Android settings, or uninstalling the App.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact 1000usefulapps@gmail.com.
